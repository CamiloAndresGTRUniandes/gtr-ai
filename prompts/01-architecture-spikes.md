# Architecture Spikes (reconstructed working brief)
Act as principal architect and technical research lead. Follow `AGENTS.md` and the master brief. Read all relevant original criteria in `docs/spikes/` and the twenty-spike closure matrix in `docs/UNBLOCK_PLAN.md`. Treat prior evidence as historical, not proof of untested integrations.

Research: inspect current environment and repository, official source/docs, versions, licenses, compatibility, security and provider restrictions; propose dependency order, budget and minimal tests. Apply: after approval, execute isolated, reproducible prototypes, not production features. Verify: reproduce, review source and raw measurements, report failures and alternatives. Archive: after approval consolidate evidence, ADRs, risks and readiness. Ask for approval between each phase. No unapproved live model or paid calls.

Compare actual dependencies and alternatives; hard security/legal/correctness gates override numerical scores. Output revised dependency baseline, risk register, reports, compatibility matrices, benchmarks, proposed ADRs and implementation readiness. No invented evidence.
